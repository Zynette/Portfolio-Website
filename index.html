01 / UNDERSTAND

Get the whole picture.

What were you trying to do? What changed? I clarify the symptom and its impact before deciding where to look.

  1. Understand the problem

    Clarify the symptom, what changed, and the person’s goal.

  2. Isolate the cause

    Separate sign-in, permissions and application behaviour. Test one assumption at a time.

  3. Resolve and verify

    Use an approved change or give the right team clear evidence. Ask the person to confirm the original task works.

  4. Document for next time

    Record the symptom, checks and resolution. Make the next investigation easier.
